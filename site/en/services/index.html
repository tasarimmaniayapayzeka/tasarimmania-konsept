FAQs

Common questions about working across five modules

The questions that come up most on choosing modules, working together and pricing.

Do I have to take all five modules?

No. Each module works on its own and is contracted on its own. You can take just the website, just ad management or just video production. Most of our clients start with one module and add the others over time.

Why do the modules cost less together?

The same discovery, the same brand file and the same schedule aren't set up twice. With separate agencies each one gets to know the brand from scratch, and each one wants its own meetings and its own brief. Under one roof discovery happens once; the images, copy and measurement setup produced in one module get reused in the next.

Will I deal with a different person for each module?

No. One team, one dashboard, one invoice. All five modules run to the same schedule and are managed from one point. When something goes wrong, the modules don't point at each other; responsibility stays with the same team.

Can I add a module later?

Yes, in any month you like. Modules are contracted independently of each other. When a new one is added, the brief and brand files from the existing work carry over — the process doesn't start from scratch.

Which module should I start with?

By looking at where the loss is. In the first call we share the screen and mark together the points where your site and your ad account are losing money. The starting module is chosen from that list, not from guesswork.
