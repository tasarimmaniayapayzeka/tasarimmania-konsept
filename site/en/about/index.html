Who we are

You're not looking for an agency — you're looking for a team

Most brands come to us with the same sentence: “We work with three separate suppliers and none of them knows what the others are doing.” The site is in one place, the advertising somewhere else, the video somewhere else again. When something goes wrong, everyone points at everyone else.

That's why we didn't grow — we merged. Web and software, mobile apps, Meta & Google Ads, video production and SEO — all five in the same office, on the same schedule, in the same report. What one module produces becomes the next one's input.

We're in Istanbul. We work remotely too, but we prefer to meet face to face the first time — opening the screen together and showing you what's losing money gets to the point faster than any presentation.

What we don't sell

We don't sell likes, impressions or “brand awareness”. Those aren't results, they're intermediate metrics. We don't promise anything we can't measure — and what we do measure, we report as it is. A bad month gets called a bad month.

We don't make claims like ranking guarantees or “first page in a month”. If someone does, be careful.

How we work

How we work, in three sentences

01

We measure first

We never start a job without measurement in place. How many people arrive and from where, which of them call, which campaign turns a profit — without knowing that, every investment is a matter of faith.

02

Accounts in your name

Google Ads, Meta, Analytics — all opened in your name. If we part ways, the data and the advertising history stay with you. Changing agency shouldn't mean starting from zero.

03

One point of contact

Even if you use all five modules, you speak to one person. You won't hear “that's not our department” from us; internal coordination is our problem, not yours.
