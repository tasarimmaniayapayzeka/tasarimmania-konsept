How does the process run, from logo to brand identity guidelines?

The logo is the brand's single visual signature, while the brand identity guidelines are a thorough set of rules describing how that signature is used in every setting.

The process starts with a discovery call that settles the brand's values and its audience. The direction is then set through concept sketches and a moodboard, and the chosen concept is produced in vector format. At this stage the colour palette, typographic hierarchy and logo variants (horizontal, vertical, mark) are defined together. The guidelines document brings the minimum size, clear space, examples of prohibited use and the colour codes together in one file. That way, different agencies or in-house teams all apply the brand to the same standard.

02

The Technical Difference Between Business Cards, Catalogues and Social Media Templates

Business card and catalogue files going to print need a CMYK colour profile, bleed and high resolution, while digital catalogues and social media templates are prepared in RGB and optimized for screens.

Depending on the number of products and the page layout, a catalogue can be delivered as a print-ready PDF or as an interactive digital format with a page-turn effect. Social media templates come in different ratios — post, story, cover image — matched to the brand's colours and typography, in editable layers. The aim is for the same brand identity to appear intact on every channel. Because pricing and delivery times vary with the package, those details are covered in the pricing section. The design delivered is a creative work; copyright law sets the extent of the usage rights.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions: graphic design and brand identity

What is a brand identity, and how is it different from just a logo?

The logo is the brand's mark, while the brand identity is a whole system covering the colour palette, typography, visual language and rules of use. The identity guidelines make sure the logo is applied consistently across every channel.

What stages does a logo go through before it's delivered?

The process runs through a discovery call, concept sketches, vector production of the chosen direction, and a final check. At delivery you get the vector source files together with the different usage formats.

What makes business card and catalogue design prices vary?

The cost is set by things like the number of pages, how complex the content is, whether illustration is needed and the delivery format. Current package details are in the pricing section.

What formats is a catalogue delivered in?

A catalogue can be prepared as a high-resolution, print-ready PDF, or as an interactive digital file with a page-turn effect on screen. Where it's needed, both formats are delivered together.

Are social media templates part of the package, or offered separately?

The template set is offered either as an add-on module to the brand identity package or as a standalone service. The scope is settled during the call, according to what the brand needs.

Can we keep our existing logo and just have brand identity guidelines produced?

Yes — the existing logo can be analysed and kept, with guidelines produced covering only colour, typography and rules of use. If the logo needs revising, that's assessed separately.
