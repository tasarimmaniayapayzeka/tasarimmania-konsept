In depth
01

What does ASO cover, from keywords to graphics?

ASO starts by arranging the signals that decide which searches the store algorithm shows the app in.

Keyword placement in the title, subtitle and description fields is built by comparing it against the keyword gaps competing apps are covering. Category choice and localisation settings are reviewed against search behaviour in the target market. Store graphics aren't an aesthetic preference; the order of the screenshots, the message in the first frames and the opening seconds of the preview video directly affect the user's decision to install. Combinations of icon, title and screenshots are compared through A/B testing and the winning variant goes live.

02

Download Campaigns, Channel Choice and Retention Analysis

Download volume after launch is built without tying it to a single channel.

Google Ads app campaigns reach users with high search intent, while Meta Ads feeds the awareness side through interest and lookalike audience targeting; the budget split is continually rebalanced against campaign results. The real measurement work doesn't end at the moment of download. Deep linking and event tracking mark the user's sign-up, first transaction and return visit as separate steps. The cohort table built from that data shows where the app is abandoned and gives a basis for the notification and in-app messaging that will improve retention. The limits of the store fields aren't left to guesswork; the App Store product page guidelines set them all out.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions: ASO and app marketing

What exactly does ASO (app store optimization) cover?

ASO covers every component of the store listing that affects search — from keyword placement to icon and screenshot design, from category choice to managing user reviews — and the keyword gaps competitors are covering are scanned regularly.

What happens after the app is published to get it downloaded?

Alongside the ASO work, a paid download campaign comes in. Campaigns set up through Google Ads and Meta Ads are structured separately around the audience's search intent and interests; the budget is redistributed between channels according to performance.

Are store graphics and the preview video charged separately?

Image production and video production are items that vary with the package chosen; the exact scope is in the pricing section. Which set of graphics is included is settled at the quote stage.

Is the download campaign run through Google Ads or Meta Ads?

We don't tie ourselves to one channel; Google Ads reaches users with high search intent, while Meta Ads focuses on broad awareness and lookalike audience targeting. The campaign mix is set by the app's category and how its audience behaves.

How do you measure how many of the people who download the app keep using it?

The event tracking we set up records the user's sign-up, first transaction and return visit as separate steps. The cohort analysis built from that data shows the retention curve and the step where the drop happens.

Can an app be found in the store without ASO work?

The app is technically listed in the store, but it isn't likely to come forward in the keyword areas competitors have optimized. An app published without ASO usually stays visible only in direct searches for the brand name.
