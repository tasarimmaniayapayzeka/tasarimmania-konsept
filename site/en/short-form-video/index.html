01

Why are the first-second edit and the narrative rhythm decisive?

In a reels edit, the first second is the moment the viewer decides whether or not to stop their thumb.

That's why several different openings are tried at the edit suite for the hook: a striking visual contrast, an unexpected movement, or a narrative that opens with a direct question. The cutting rhythm backs that decision up; the pace of transition between scenes is set by the density of the message, and any dead space is cleared out. The text layers are placed in sync with the sound, so the message isn't lost when the content is watched with the sound off. In keeping with the nature of the short format, the narrative structure is built around a single idea; a clear call is the aim, rather than a scattered message. This approach sets out to hold the viewer to the end, in organic posts and in advertising alike.

02

Safe Zone, Format Compatibility and Royalty-Free Music

The vertical format carries areas at the top and the bottom of the screen that clash with the platform interface; the profile icon, the description text and the interaction buttons all sit in those regions.

During the edit, the headline, the subtitles and the brand logo are placed outside that safe zone; otherwise an important part of the message can end up beneath the interface elements. With the music, the copyright position is checked in advance; the selection is made from the platform's licensed library or from tracks whose commercial rights are clear, so the risk of a restriction or of the audio being stripped after publication is headed off. The format is optimized technically for the platform too; the resolution, the frame rate and the file compression are set so they don't degrade the published quality. The result is content that fits the platform's technical requirements both visually and audibly. The music used is a copyright matter; Türkiye's Directorate General of Copyright sets out the route to permission.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions: Instagram Reels production

How long should a video be for Instagram Reels?

The exact duration varies with the density of the message, the strength of the hook and the audience retention rate being aimed at. The edit team settles the format decision according to the content strategy, and the duration is shared in the quote and in the pricing section.

Why does the safe zone matter in Reels, and what happens if it's ignored?

The safe zone is the set of regions where the interface elements sit on top of the video. If those areas are ignored, the headline, the logo or the subtitles end up beneath the profile details, the description text or the interaction buttons and become unreadable, which weakens the effect of the message.

Can the music used in a reel create a copyright problem?

Music that isn't chosen from the right source can end in an audio restriction or in the content being taken down after publication. To head that risk off, the tracks used are ones whose commercial rights are clear, or ones chosen from the platform's licensed library.

Can the same reel be used on Instagram and TikTok in the same way?

Because the formats are similar, the basic edit can usually be adapted; but each platform has its own safe-zone limits, its own recommendation algorithm and its own viewing habits. So before republishing, the text placement and the opening second are checked against the platform.

Is a reel published organically, or straight as an advert?

The content can be cut for organic posting and for advertising alike; the edit, the copy and the call elements differ according to the goal. In versions meant for advertising, clarity of message and the call to action are brought to the front.

Can a video in reels format also be used on horizontal channels?

A vertical edit isn't directly suited to horizontal channels, because the framing and the focal point are planned differently. Where horizontal use is needed, the content is reframed, or multi-format planning is done at the original shoot stage.
