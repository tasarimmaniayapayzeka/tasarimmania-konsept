Account Management and Audience Segmentation

Campaign work starts with the ad account set up in Meta Business Manager.

The pixel and Conversions API integration measures visitor behaviour and feeds data into campaign optimization. The audience is built in three layers: custom audiences drawn from existing customer lists, lookalike audiences resembling those, and broad audiences based on interests. The campaign structure is split by funnel stage; awareness, consideration and conversion goals are run in separate campaigns, and each stage's bidding strategy is optimized independently.

02

Which ad format suits which goal, and how is retargeting set up?

The format is chosen according to the nature of the product or service.

A static image is strong for getting a message across quickly, and the collection format suits showing several products; short video and the Reels placement have high attention-grabbing power but need more production. The decision is made by weighing past campaign data and creative production capacity together. In the retargeting layer, site visitors, video viewers and users who didn't complete the basket are split into separate groups; each group is targeted again with a message matched to its level of interest. Advertising regulations apply on social channels too; commercial advertising rules make no distinction by platform.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions: Meta Ads management

Whose name is the ad account for a Meta Ads campaign opened in?

The ad account is opened in the brand's name, under the business's own Meta Business Manager; the agency runs the campaigns with administrator access. That way ownership of the account always stays with the business.

What happens when a Meta ad account is restricted or disabled?

A restriction usually comes from a policy breach, a payment problem or a suspicious activity flag. The reason is established, an appeal is started, the item flagged as a breach is corrected, and the interruption to the campaign is kept to a minimum.

Which ad format suits our business, and how is that decided?

The format decision is made by looking at the range of products, the capacity to produce creative, and the platform habits of the target audience. The decision is validated with small-scale test campaigns.

How does Meta's ad review work, and why might an ad not be running?

Every ad goes through Meta's automated review, and a manual one where necessary, before going live; that process is outside the agency's control. Rejection usually comes from prohibited content, a misleading claim or an inconsistency with the landing page; the item is corrected and the ad resubmitted.

How is retargeting set up, and which visitors see it?

Retargeting is built on data from the pixel placed on the website and the Conversions API. People who viewed a page, looked at a product or didn't complete the basket are split into separate groups and shown again with an offer that matches their behaviour.

Which metrics does the Meta ad report include?

The report covers the core metrics: reach, impressions, click-through rate, number of conversions and cost per result. Added to those are ROAS, which shows the return on ad spend, and breakdowns by audience.

    The pixel placed on the website and the server-side Conversions API together let conversion data be measured accurately without being affected by browser blockers; they're the foundation of retargeting and optimization.

    The product catalogue is connected to Commerce Manager to set up dynamic ads that automatically show a user the product they visited or added to their basket; a wide product range is managed without producing each ad by hand.
