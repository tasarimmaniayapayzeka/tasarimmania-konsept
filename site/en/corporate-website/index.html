01

How Are the Page Architecture and Information Hierarchy Built?

On a corporate site the number of pages isn't decided arbitrarily; the breadth of the service and product groups, the variety of audience segments and the different information needs of decision-makers all shape that number.

First we map a site structure together with your corporate communications team, settling the main menu headings, the sub-menus and the deep categories that call for a mega menu. Every service or product group gets its own landing page, while corporate pages (leadership, values, sustainability), the careers section, the press room and investor relations sit in a separate layer of the hierarchy. The breadcrumb structure and internal link strategy are built so the visitor never loses their bearings on any page. That way a large volume of content becomes easy to navigate rather than complicated.

02

Admin Panel, Brand Identity and System Integrations

A multi-page structure demands a strong content management panel; we set up role-based permissions so the marketing, HR and sales teams can update their own sections without getting in each other's way.

The colour, typography and logo rules from your brand identity guidelines are built into a predefined component library in the panel, so brand consistency holds whichever department adds the content. A blog and news module lets company announcements and sector content be published regularly, while schema markup, corporate data (Organization schema), an XML sitemap and canonical tags help search engines read the site architecture correctly. For multinational companies we set up a language-based hreflang structure. If you need integration with existing systems such as a CRM, an application form, a dealer locator or an ERP, we build those connections into the architecture from the start. The foundation of search visibility on a corporate site is described in Google's own starter guide .

FAQs

Frequently asked questions: corporate website scope and process

How many pages does a corporate website have, and what sets the number?

There's no fixed number of pages; it's set project by project, according to the range of your services and products, your audience segments and your corporate communications needs. In the discovery call we map the site structure together first, then settle the scope.

What's the difference between a corporate website and a single-page landing page?

A landing page is a single-flow structure focused on one offer. A corporate site carries a multi-layered information architecture that serves several service groups and different visitor profiles; it aims for visibility across a broad set of topics in search.

Which pages must a corporate site have?

About, service or product pages, case studies, careers, contact and the privacy pages form the backbone of a corporate site. Depending on the sector, pages like a press room, investor relations or a dealer network are added to that backbone.

How is a corporate website made findable on Google?

A sound page hierarchy, a clean internal link structure, schema markup and a fast technical foundation — combined with a content strategy — position the site as a credible source in search. Run alongside our SEO service, the result lasts longer.

Is it more sensible to refresh my existing corporate site or rebuild it from scratch?

That depends on the existing site's technical foundation and the volume of its content; a short audit establishes together which route is more efficient. The detail is shared in the pricing section.

Who prepares the content (copy, photographs) for a corporate website?

We build the copy with you, keeping your corporate tone; you share the raw information and your brand guidelines if you have them. On the visual side you can use your existing corporate photographs, or we can bring professional photography and image production into the project where it's needed.
